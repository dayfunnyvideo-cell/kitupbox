📖 What is an Expense Tracker?

An Expense Tracker is a simple tool that helps you record and monitor your daily spending. By tracking where your money goes, you can identify spending patterns, cut unnecessary costs, and save more.

Our Expense Tracker saves your entries locally in your browser — no data is sent to any server.

✨ Key Features

  • 100% Free — No signup, no charges
  • Categorize Expenses — 8 categories to choose from
  • Auto Total — Running total updates automatically
  • Saved Locally — Data stays on your device
  • Easy Delete — Remove individual expenses
  • Works Everywhere — Mobile, tablet, desktop

🎯 How to Use Expense Tracker

  1. Enter the description of your expense
  2. Enter the amount
  3. Select a category
  4. Click "Add Expense"
  5. See your running total and list

💡 Why Track Expenses?

  • Know exactly where your money goes
  • Identify unnecessary spending
  • Plan a better monthly budget
  • Save more for financial goals
  • Reduce financial stress
  • Make better spending decisions

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data saved permanently?
Your expenses are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay even if you refresh, but clear if you clear browser data.
Can I use it offline?
Yes! Once the page loads, you can use it entirely offline.
Is my financial data safe?
Yes, 100%. All data stays on your device. We never see or store anything.
Can I export my expenses?
Currently, no export feature. It may be added in a future update.
How do I delete an expense?
Click the small "Delete" button next to any expense. To remove all, use "Clear All".
Does it work on mobile?
Yes! Works perfectly on Android, iPhone, tablets, and desktop.
