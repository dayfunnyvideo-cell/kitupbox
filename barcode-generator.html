📖 What is a Barcode?

A barcode is a machine-readable representation of data in the form of parallel lines of varying widths. Barcodes are used everywhere — from product packaging to inventory management to event tickets.

Our Barcode Generator creates high-quality barcodes in multiple formats, entirely in your browser. No data is uploaded to any server.

🎯 Supported Barcode Formats

  • CODE128 — Any text or numbers (most versatile)
  • EAN-13 — Standard retail barcode (13 digits)
  • EAN-8 — Small retail products (8 digits)
  • UPC — North American retail (12 digits)
  • CODE39 — Industrial use (letters + numbers)
  • ITF-14 — Shipping containers (14 digits)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which format should I use?
For general text/numbers, use CODE128. For retail products, use EAN-13 or UPC. For industrial, CODE39 works best.
Do barcodes expire?
No! Barcodes are static and never expire. They work forever.
Can I use these commercially?
Yes! The barcodes are yours to use for any purpose — personal or commercial.
Why does my barcode fail with certain text?
Each format has its own rules. EAN-13 needs exactly 13 digits, UPC needs 12. Use CODE128 for any text.
How do I scan a barcode?
Use any barcode scanner app or hardware scanner. Most modern phone cameras can also scan barcodes.
Is it free?
Yes, 100% free with no limits. Create as many barcodes as you want.
