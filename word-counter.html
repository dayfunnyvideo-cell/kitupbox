📖 What is a Word Counter?

A Word Counter is a tool that counts the number of words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in a piece of text. It's essential for writers, students, bloggers, and SEO professionals.

✨ Key Features

  • Real-time Counting — Updates as you type
  • Complete Stats — Words, chars, sentences, paragraphs
  • Reading Time — Estimated based on 200 WPM
  • Free Forever — No signup, no limits
  • Privacy First — Nothing uploaded

🎯 When to Use Word Counter?

  • Students — Essays with word limits
  • Bloggers — SEO-optimized articles
  • Writers — Novel and story chapters
  • Journalists — News articles with word counts
  • Social Media — Tweets, captions, posts
  • SEO — Meta descriptions, titles
  • Academic — Thesis and research papers

💡 Reading Time Calculation

We estimate reading time based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute. This is the standard used by most publishers and content platforms.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is word count calculated?
We split your text by spaces and count each group of characters as one word. Standard word counting method.
Does it count numbers as words?
Yes, numbers count as words. For example, "I have 3 cats" = 4 words.
What counts as a sentence?
A sentence ends with a period (.), question mark (?), or exclamation mark (!).
How is reading time calculated?
We divide total words by 200 (average reading speed) to estimate minutes.
Is my text saved?
No! Everything is processed in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.
Is there a text limit?
No specific limit. Paste as much text as you want.
