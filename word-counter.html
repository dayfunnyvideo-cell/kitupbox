📖 What is a word counter?

A word counter counts the words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in a piece of text. Writers use it to stay inside a limit, such as an essay with a maximum length or a post with a character cap. This one updates the numbers as you type or paste, so you always see where you stand.

✨ What this word counter shows

  • Words: every group of characters separated by spaces or line breaks.
  • Characters: all characters, including spaces and line breaks.
  • No spaces: characters without any spaces or line breaks.
  • Sentences and paragraphs: counted from punctuation and blank lines.
  • Reading time and speaking time: how long the text takes to read silently or to say out loud.

How to use the word counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Read the counts below the box. They change as you edit.
  3. Use Copy Text to copy it, or Clear to start again.

💡 How the numbers are worked out

  • Words: the text is split wherever there is a space or a line break, and each piece counts as one word. So "I have 3 cats" is 4 words, and a hyphenated word such as "well-known" is 1 word.
  • Sentences: a sentence is counted when it ends with a period, question mark or exclamation mark followed by a space or the end of the text. A number such as 3.5 does not end a sentence.
  • Paragraphs: blocks of text separated by a blank line.
  • Reading time: words divided by 200 words per minute. Adults often read somewhere around 200 to 250 words per minute, so this is a safe average. A 1,000 word article takes about 5 minutes to read.
  • Speaking time: words divided by about 130 words per minute, a common pace for speaking aloud. A 1,000 word speech takes about 8 minutes.

🎯 Common word and character limits

Limits depend on the school, the app and the site, and they can change, so always check the rules you are given. These are common examples:

  • College application essay: the Common App personal essay allows up to 650 words.
  • Post on X (Twitter): 280 characters for most accounts.
  • Instagram caption: up to 2,200 characters.
  • Text message (SMS): 160 characters fit in one standard message.
  • Page title for search results: about 60 characters before it may be cut off.
  • Meta description: about 150 to 160 characters before it may be cut off.

✍️ Who uses a word counter

  • Students: essays and papers with a word limit.
  • Bloggers and marketers: articles, titles and descriptions.
  • Writers: chapters and stories.
  • Speakers: checking how long a talk will run.
  • Social media users: captions and posts with a character cap.

Tips for staying within a limit

  • Write the first draft without counting, then trim.
  • Cut filler such as "very", "really" and "in order to".
  • Replace long phrases with one clear word.
  • Remove repeated points, and keep the strongest example.

Different programs count a little differently, for example with symbols or dashes standing alone. If a strict limit matters, check your count in the program or form that will receive the text.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is word count calculated?
The text is split at every space and line break, and each piece counts as one word. This is the usual method for counting words.
Does it count numbers as words?
Yes. A number counts as a word, so "I have 3 cats" is 4 words.
What is the difference between characters and characters without spaces?
Characters counts everything, including spaces and line breaks. The no-spaces count leaves out spaces and line breaks. Some forms ask for one and some for the other.
What counts as a sentence?
A sentence is counted when text ends with a period, question mark or exclamation mark followed by a space or the end of the text.
How is reading time calculated?
We divide the number of words by 200, an average adult reading speed in words per minute. Speaking time uses about 130 words per minute.
Does it work for other languages?
It works for languages that put spaces between words, such as English, Spanish and Hindi. Languages written without spaces between words, such as Chinese and Japanese, need a character count instead.
Is my text saved?
No. The counting runs in your browser, and this page does not send or save what you type.
Is there a text limit?
This page has no set limit. Very large texts may make your browser slow.
