📖 What is PDF to Word?

PDF to Word is a free online tool that extracts text from PDF files and creates editable Word documents (.docx). Perfect for when you need to edit content locked inside a PDF.

Our tool processes everything locally in your browser — your PDF files never leave your device.

✨ Key Features

  • 100% Free — No signup, no charges
  • Editable Output — Creates .docx files
  • Fast Processing — Convert in seconds
  • Secure & Private — Files stay on your device
  • Works Everywhere — All devices

🎯 How to Use PDF to Word

  1. Select a PDF file
  2. Click "Convert to Word & Download"
  3. Your Word file downloads automatically

💡 When Should You Use It?

  • Editing text from a locked PDF
  • Repurposing PDF content
  • Extracting text for translation
  • Creating new documents from old PDFs

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does it preserve formatting?
We extract text content, which may not preserve complex formatting like tables and images. Basic text and paragraphs are preserved.
Is my PDF safe?
Yes, 100%. Your PDF is processed locally in your browser — never uploaded to any server.
What is the output format?
The output is a .docx file which can be opened in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or any modern word processor.
Can I convert scanned PDFs?
For scanned PDFs (which are images), use our Image OCR tool instead. This tool works best with text-based PDFs.
Is there a size limit?
No specific limit, but we recommend PDFs under 20 MB for best performance.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes! Works perfectly on Android, iPhone, tablets, and desktop.
