📖 What is Image Compressor?

An Image Compressor reduces the file size of an image while keeping acceptable visual quality. Smaller images load faster on websites, send easily via email, and use less storage.

✨ Key Features

  • Adjustable Quality — 10% to 100%
  • Resize Option — Limit max width
  • Instant Preview — See result before download
  • Shows Savings — % size reduced
  • No Quality Loss — Smart compression
  • 100% Free — No signup, no limits
  • Privacy First — All in browser

🎯 When to Use Image Compressor?

  • Uploading images to websites (faster loading)
  • Sending photos via email (attachment limits)
  • Posting on social media (faster uploads)
  • Saving storage space on your device
  • Preparing images for e-commerce
  • Optimizing blog images for SEO

💡 Recommended Settings

  • Web images — 80% quality, 1920px max width
  • Email photos — 70% quality, 1280px max width
  • Thumbnails — 80% quality, 400px max width
  • Print quality — 95% quality, no resize

❓ Why Use KitUpBox Image Compressor?

Fast, free, and privacy-focused. Your images never leave your device — compression happens entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Will compression reduce quality?
At 80%+ quality, most people can't tell the difference. For maximum savings, use lower quality (60-70%).
What's the best quality setting?
80% is the sweet spot for most web images. It reduces size significantly with minimal quality loss.
Which formats are supported?
JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and most common image formats.
Is my image uploaded to a server?
No! Everything is processed locally in your browser. Your image never leaves your device.
Can I compress multiple images?
Currently one at a time. Batch compression may come in future updates.
What output format is used?
Images are saved as JPEG for best compression. PNG transparency will be converted to white background.
