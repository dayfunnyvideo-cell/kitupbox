📖 What is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator finds your exact age based on your date of birth. It calculates years, months, days, hours, and even minutes you've lived. Perfect for official forms, birthday planning, or just curiosity.

✨ Key Features

  • Exact Age — Years, months, days
  • Total Time — Days, hours, minutes
  • Next Birthday — Days remaining
  • Day of Week — What day you were born
  • Custom Date — Calculate age for any date
  • Free Forever — No signup required

🎯 When to Use Age Calculator?

  • Filling official forms accurately
  • Checking eligibility (jobs, exams, schemes)
  • Planning birthday parties
  • Curiosity about exact age
  • Calculating baby's age in months
  • Legal age verification

💡 Interesting Facts

  • Average human lives ~80 years = ~700,000 hours
  • You've lived ~29,000 days by age 80
  • You've taken ~670 million breaths by 80

❓ Why Use KitUpBox Age Calculator?

Accurate, fast, and completely free. Handles leap years correctly. Everything runs in your browser.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated?
We calculate the exact difference between your birth date and today (or a custom date), accounting for leap years.
Does it handle leap years?
Yes! Our calculation accounts for February 29th in leap years.
Can I calculate age for a future date?
Yes, use the "Calculate Age As Of" field to set any date.
Is my birth date stored?
No! Everything is calculated in your browser. We don't store any data.
Is it accurate for official use?
Yes, the calculation is accurate. However, always verify with official documents for legal purposes.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes! Works perfectly on all devices.
