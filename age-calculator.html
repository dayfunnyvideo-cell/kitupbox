📖 What is an age calculator?

An age calculator finds the time between your date of birth and a second date, which is today unless you choose another one. It shows your age in years, months and days, and also as total months, weeks, days, hours and minutes. People use it for forms, school and sports cutoff dates, a baby's age in months, or just to see how long they have been around.

✨ What this calculator shows

  • Exact age: full years, months and days.
  • Totals: months, weeks, days, hours and minutes since birth.
  • Next birthday: how many days are left.
  • Day of the week: the day you were born.
  • Any date: use "Calculate Age As Of" to find age on a past or future date.

How to use the age calculator

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Leave "Calculate Age As Of" on today, or choose another date.
  3. Press Calculate Age to see your result.

💡 How age is worked out

The calculator subtracts the birth date from the second date in three steps: years, then months, then days. If the day of the month comes out negative, it borrows the number of days from the previous month and takes one month away. If the months come out negative, it borrows 12 months from the years.

Example: someone born on March 15, 1990 on November 17, 2026 is 36 years, 8 months and 2 days old. That is 440 months, 1,913 weeks or 13,396 days in total.

Another example: someone born on August 28, 1995 on November 17, 2026 is 31 years, 2 months and 20 days old, which is 11,404 days.

A baby born on May 10, 2024 is 2 years, 6 months and 7 days old on November 17, 2026. That is 30 months in total, which is the number many doctors use for young children.

🎯 When an exact age matters

  • School enrollment, where a child often must reach a certain age by a cutoff date set by the district or state
  • Youth sports leagues, which group children by age on a set date
  • Driver's license, voting and other age limits, which differ by state and by rule
  • Retirement and benefit planning, where the age you reach on a date changes what you qualify for
  • Tracking a baby's age in months for check-ups
  • Planning a milestone birthday

🎂 Leap day birthdays

People born on February 29 have a birthday only in leap years. In other years, this calculator counts their next birthday as March 1. Some places and organizations treat February 28 as the birthday instead, so check the rule of the office or form you are dealing with.

This calculator is for general use. For legal or official purposes, rely on the documents and rules of the organization that asks for your age.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated from a date of birth?
Subtract the birth date from the second date: first the years, then the months, then the days. When the days or months go below zero, borrow from the next larger unit.
How do I calculate my age in months?
Multiply the full years by 12 and add the extra months. This calculator shows it as Total Months. For a baby born in May 2024, that is 30 months in November 2026.
Does it handle leap years?
Yes. Total days include February 29 in leap years, so the day counts are correct.
What happens if I was born on February 29?
You have a birthday only in leap years. In other years this calculator counts your next birthday as March 1. Rules differ by place, so check with the office or form.
Can I find my age on a past or future date?
Yes. Change the "Calculate Age As Of" date to any day, for example a school cutoff date or the day of an event.
Is my birth date stored?
No. The calculation runs in your browser when you press the button. This page does not save the dates you choose.
Is it accurate for official use?
The calculation is accurate for general use. For legal or official needs, follow the rules and documents of the organization that asks for your age.
