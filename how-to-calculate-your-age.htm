How to Calculate Your Age (Complete Guide)

3 min read · Nov 17, 2026

Your age is the time that has passed since you were born. Most of the time a quick subtraction is enough. Sometimes you need the exact number of years, months and days, for a form, a school cutoff date or just for fun. This guide shows both ways.

The quick way: subtract the years

Age = Current year − Birth year (then check your birthday)

If your birthday has not happened yet this year, subtract 1.

Example 1: Born March 15, 1990. On November 17, 2026: 2026 − 1990 = 36. The birthday has passed, so the age is 36.
Example 2: Born December 20, 1990. On November 17, 2026: 2026 − 1990 = 36. The birthday has not happened yet, so the age is 35.

Exact age in years, months and days

  1. Years: count the full years from the birth date to your most recent birthday.
  2. Months: count the full months from that birthday to today.
  3. Days: count the days left over.
Example: Born March 15, 1990. On November 17, 2026, the age is 36 years, 8 months and 2 days. March 15, 2026 is the latest birthday. From there, 8 months takes you to November 15, and 2 more days reach November 17.
Example with borrowed days: Born August 28, 1995. On November 17, 2026, the age is 31 years, 2 months and 20 days. August 28, 2026 is the latest birthday. Two months later is October 28. From October 28 to November 17 is 20 days.

How many months or days old

To find your age in months, multiply the full years by 12 and add the extra months. For 36 years and 8 months, that is 36 × 12 + 8 = 440 months.

Counting days is harder because years have 365 or 366 days. The easiest way is to count the days between the two dates. Someone born on March 15, 1990 is 13,396 days old on November 17, 2026.

Birthdays on February 29

A person born on a leap day has a real birthday only every four years. In other years many people celebrate on February 28 or March 1. For legal purposes the rule can differ from place to place.

When exact age matters

  • School enrollment cutoff dates
  • Sports and activity age groups
  • Insurance, benefits and retirement planning
  • Medical and travel forms

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting the birthday check. Subtracting years alone can be off by one.
  • Mixing up date formats. 03/04/1990 means March 4 in the United States but 3 April in many other countries.
  • Treating every month as 30 days. Months have 28 to 31 days, so count real dates.
  • Using the wrong "today". To find an age on a past or future date, use that date, not the current one.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my birth year from my age?

Subtract your age from the current year. The answer can be one year off, depending on whether your birthday has passed this year.

How do I calculate my age on a future date?

Use the same steps, but treat the future date as "today".

Why does my age differ between two calculators?

Usually one counts a leap day or a month end differently. For official use, follow the rule of the office or form.
