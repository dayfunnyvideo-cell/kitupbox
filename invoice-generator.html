What is an invoice?

An invoice is a bill that you send to a customer for work you did or things you sold. It lists what you provided, how much it costs, and when and how to pay. A clear invoice helps you get paid on time and gives both sides a record. This free invoice generator lets you fill in the details and print the invoice or save it as a PDF.

How to make an invoice

  1. Enter your business name, contact details and address.
  2. Enter your client's name and address.
  3. Set an invoice number, the invoice date and the due date.
  4. Add each item with a description, quantity and rate. The amounts and totals are worked out for you.
  5. Add a discount or tax rate if you need one, and write any payment notes.
  6. Press Print or Save as PDF. In the print window, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to get a file you can email.

What to include on an invoice

  • The word "Invoice" and a unique invoice number
  • Your business name, address and contact details
  • Your client's name and address
  • The invoice date and the due date
  • A list of the work or items, with quantity, rate and amount
  • The subtotal, any discount or tax, and the total due
  • Payment terms and how to pay, such as bank transfer details or a payment link

How the totals are worked out

Each line amount is quantity × rate. The subtotal is the sum of all lines. A discount is taken from the subtotal first, and tax is then worked out on the discounted amount. The total due is the discounted amount plus tax.

Example: 10 hours of web design at $50 is $500, and one logo at $150 brings the subtotal to $650. With 8% tax, the tax is $52 and the total is $702. If you give a 10% discount, the discount is $65, the amount after discount is $585, the tax at 8% is $46.80 and the total is $631.80.

Tips for getting paid faster

  • Send the invoice as soon as the work is done.
  • Give each invoice its own number, and keep the numbers in order.
  • State the due date clearly. "Net 30" means payment is due 30 days after the invoice date.
  • Make it easy to pay by saying exactly how.
  • Keep a copy of every invoice for your records.

This tool helps you format an invoice. It does not give legal or tax advice. Sales tax rules for services and goods depend on where you and your customer are, so ask a tax professional if you are not sure what to charge.

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator free?
Yes. You can make and print as many invoices as you like, with no sign-up.
How do I save my invoice as a PDF?
Press Print or Save as PDF. In the window that opens, choose "Save as PDF" as the destination, then save the file. On a phone, use the share or print option in your browser.
Is my invoice saved?
No. Everything stays in your browser, and nothing is saved. If you close or reload the page, the invoice is gone, so save the PDF before you leave.
How do I add tax to an invoice?
Enter your tax rate in the Tax box. It is applied to the amount after any discount. Tax rules differ by place, so check what applies to you.
What does Net 30 mean?
Net 30 means the customer should pay within 30 days of the invoice date. Net 15 means 15 days, and so on.
Can I add my logo?
Not at the moment. You can put your business name at the top of the invoice.
Can I use a different currency?
Yes. Pick the currency from the list. The amounts are shown in that currency, but no conversion is done.
Is this a legal document?
An invoice is a request for payment and a record of the sale. This tool only helps you format it. For legal or tax questions, talk to a professional.
