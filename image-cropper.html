📖 What is Image Cropper?

An Image Cropper lets you cut out a specific portion of an image. Perfect for removing unwanted parts, focusing on a subject, or matching required dimensions for social media and websites.

✨ Key Features

  • Preset Ratios — 1:1, 16:9, 4:3, 9:16, 3:4, 3:2
  • Free Crop — Any custom size
  • Drag & Position — Move the crop area
  • Instant Preview — See the crop box
  • High Quality — Preserves resolution
  • 100% Free — No signup, no limits

🎯 When to Use Image Cropper?

  • Preparing profile pictures (1:1)
  • Creating Instagram posts (1:1)
  • Instagram Stories (9:16)
  • YouTube thumbnails (16:9)
  • Facebook covers (16:9)
  • Removing unwanted areas
  • Focusing on a subject

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between cropping and resizing?
Cropping cuts a portion of the image (changes what's visible). Resizing changes the dimensions (stretches or shrinks the whole image).
How do I move the crop area?
Click and drag the blue crop box to position it anywhere on your image.
Can I crop to a custom size?
Yes! Choose the "Free" ratio and drag the crop box to any size you want.
Does it preserve quality?
Yes! The cropped image retains the original resolution of the cropped area.
Is my image safe?
Yes, 100%. Everything is processed in your browser — never uploaded.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes! Works on Android, iPhone, and tablets.
