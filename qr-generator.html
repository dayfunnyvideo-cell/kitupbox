📖 What is a QR Code?

QR Code (Quick Response Code) is a 2D barcode that can store information like URLs, text, phone numbers, and more. When scanned with a smartphone camera, it instantly opens the stored information.

Our QR Code Generator is free and works entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server.

🎯 How to Use QR Generator

💡 What Can You Store in a QR Code?

  • Website URLs
  • Plain text messages
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Wi-Fi passwords
  • Business card info (vCard)
  • Location coordinates

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do QR codes expire?
No! The QR codes generated here are static and never expire. They work forever.
Can I change the color of the QR code?
Currently we generate standard black-and-white QR codes for maximum scannability. Colored QR codes may come in a future update.
Is there a limit on how many QR codes I can create?
No limit! Create as many QR codes as you want — completely free.
How do I scan the QR code?
Open your smartphone camera, point it at the QR code, and tap the notification that appears. Or use any QR scanner app.
Can I use these QR codes commercially?
Yes! The QR codes are yours to use for any purpose — personal or commercial.
What's the maximum text length?
QR codes can store up to ~4000 characters, but shorter text produces cleaner, easier-to-scan codes.
