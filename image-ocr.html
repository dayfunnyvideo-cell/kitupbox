📖 What is Image OCR?

Image OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is a technology that converts text from images into editable, searchable text. Our free online OCR tool uses AI to accurately extract text from photos, scanned documents, screenshots, and more.

Whether you have a photo of a printed page, a screenshot of an article, or a scanned receipt — our OCR tool converts it to text instantly. It works entirely in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

  • Secure & Private — Images never leave your browser

🎯 How to Use Image OCR

  1. Click on "Choose Files" button above
  2. Select an image with text (JPG, PNG)
  3. Click "Extract Text"
  4. Wait a few seconds for AI to process
  5. Copy the extracted text using the button

💡 When Should You Use Image OCR?

  • Digitizing printed documents and books
  • Extracting text from screenshots
  • Converting scanned PDFs into editable text
  • Copying text from photos (notifications, signs)
  • Reading text from receipts and invoices
  • Translating text from images
  • Getting text from handwritten notes (limited)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which image formats are supported?
We support all common image formats including JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, and more. Simply select your image and the OCR will process it.
Which languages are supported?
Our OCR tool supports English (default). It can also detect and extract text in many Latin-based languages with decent accuracy.
How accurate is the OCR?
Accuracy depends on image quality. Clear, high-resolution images with printed text give the best results (95%+). Handwritten text and blurry images may have lower accuracy.
Is my image safe?
Yes, 100%. Your image is processed directly in your browser and is never uploaded to any server. Complete privacy guaranteed.
Can I OCR handwritten text?
Our OCR works best with printed text. Handwritten text recognition is limited and may not be 100% accurate.
Does it work on mobile phones?
Absolutely! KitUpBox Image OCR works perfectly on Android, iPhone, tablets, and desktop computers. No app installation needed.
