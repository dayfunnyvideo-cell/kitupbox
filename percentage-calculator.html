📖 What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. It's one of the most common ways to compare quantities, express discounts, calculate interest, and analyze data.

✨ Three Ways to Use This Tool

  • What is X% of Y? — Find the value of a percentage (e.g., 25% of 200 = 50)
  • X is what % of Y? — Find the percentage (e.g., 50 is 25% of 200)
  • Increase/Decrease — Calculate change between two values

🎯 Common Percentage Formulas

  • X% of Y = (X/100) × Y
  • X is what % of Y = (X/Y) × 100
  • % Increase = ((New − Old) / Old) × 100
  • % Decrease = ((Old − New) / Old) × 100

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate X% of Y?
Multiply Y by X and divide by 100. Example: 25% of 200 = (25 × 200) / 100 = 50.
How do I find what percent X is of Y?
Divide X by Y and multiply by 100. Example: 50 is what percent of 200? (50/200) × 100 = 25%.
How do I calculate percentage increase?
Subtract old from new, divide by old, multiply by 100. Example: from 100 to 150 = ((150-100)/100) × 100 = 50% increase.
Can percentages be greater than 100?
Yes! For example, if something doubles, that's a 100% increase, making the new value 200% of the original.
