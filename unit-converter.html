📖 What is a Unit Converter?

A Unit Converter helps you convert measurements between different units — like meters to feet, kilograms to pounds, or Celsius to Fahrenheit. Essential for students, engineers, cooks, and travelers.

✨ Supported Categories

  • Length — Meter, Kilometer, Mile, Foot, Inch, CM, MM, Yard
  • Weight — Kilogram, Gram, Pound, Ounce, Ton
  • Temperature — Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
  • Area — Square Meter, Square Feet, Acre, Hectare
  • Volume — Liter, Milliliter, Gallon, Cup
  • Speed — km/h, mph, m/s, knots
  • Time — Second, Minute, Hour, Day

🎯 When to Use Unit Converter?

  • Students solving physics/math problems
  • Cooks converting recipe measurements
  • Travelers understanding foreign measurements
  • Engineers working with different standards
  • Fitness enthusiasts tracking weight
  • Shopping internationally
