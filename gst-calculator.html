📖 What is GST?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a single indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. It replaced multiple taxes like VAT, Service Tax, and Excise Duty, simplifying the tax structure.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between adding and removing GST?
Adding GST means calculating tax on a base amount. Removing GST means finding the base amount when you already know the total (with GST included).
Which GST rate should I use?
It depends on the product or service. Most goods and services fall under 18%. Food items are usually 5%, luxury items 28%.
