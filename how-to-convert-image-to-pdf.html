How to Convert Images to PDF (Complete Guide)

Whether you have a bunch of scanned receipts, photos of a whiteboard, or a series of screenshots, turning them into a single PDF file is a great way to keep things organized.

In this guide, we will show you how to convert your images (JPG, PNG) into one clean PDF using KitUpBox.

Why Convert Images to PDF?

  • Better Organization: Combine 20 photos into one neat document.
  • Professional Sharing: PDFs are the standard for business and academic documents.
  • Save Space: One PDF is easier to manage than a folder full of images.

Step-by-Step Guide

Our Image to PDF tool is completely free and processes everything in your browser (your images stay private).

  1. Open the Tool: Go to the Image to PDF page.
  2. Upload Images: Click "Choose Files" and select all the images you want to convert. You can select multiple images at once.
  3. Convert: Click the "Convert to PDF & Download" button.
  4. Download: The PDF will be generated and downloaded automatically.
