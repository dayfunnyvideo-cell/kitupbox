How to Compress Images Without Losing Quality

Large image files can slow down your website, clog up your email inbox, and take up too much space on your phone. However, compressing images often ruins their quality, making them look blurry or pixelated. Is there a way to reduce file size without losing sharpness?

Yes! In this guide, we will show you how to optimize your images properly.

Why Do Images Lose Quality?

When you compress an image using basic tools, the software often removes too much data. This is especially noticeable in images with lots of colors or fine details. Professional web designers carefully balance the compression level to keep images sharp while making the file size smaller.

Tips for the Best Image Quality

  • Use the Right Format: Use JPG for photographs and PNG for graphics with transparency.
  • Don't Resize Too Much: If you need a small image, start with a high-resolution original.
  • Keep a Backup: Always keep the original uncompressed file just in case.

Frequently Asked Questions
