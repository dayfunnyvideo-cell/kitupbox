📖 What is Calorie Calculator?

A Calorie Calculator estimates how many calories you need daily to maintain, lose, or gain weight. It uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, the most accurate BMR formula available.

✨ Key Features

  • BMR Calculation — Your base calorie needs
  • TDEE — Total daily energy expenditure
  • Weight Goals — Lose, maintain, or gain
  • Macro Breakdown — Protein, carbs, fat suggestions
  • Accurate Formula — Mifflin-St Jeor equation
  • 100% Free — No signup required

🎯 Understanding BMR vs TDEE

  • BMR — Calories your body burns at rest (breathing, heartbeat, etc.)
  • TDEE — BMR + calories burned through daily activity and exercise

💡 Activity Levels Explained

  • Sedentary — Desk job, minimal exercise
  • Lightly Active — 1-3 days of light exercise
  • Moderately Active — 3-5 days of moderate exercise
  • Very Active — 6-7 days of hard exercise
  • Extra Active — Athletes, physical labor jobs

🎯 Goal-Based Calorie Adjustment

  • Lose Weight — Eat 500 cal less (≈0.5 kg/week loss)
  • Maintain — Eat at TDEE level
  • Gain Weight — Eat 500 cal more (≈0.5 kg/week gain)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this calculator?
It uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, which is accurate within 5-10% for most people. Individual metabolism varies.
What's the difference between BMR and TDEE?
BMR is calories at complete rest. TDEE includes all your daily activity. TDEE is always higher than BMR.
Is 500 cal deficit safe?
Yes, a 500 cal deficit is generally considered safe and leads to ~0.5 kg loss per week. Don't go below 1200 (women) or 1500 (men) calories.
How do I use macro recommendations?
The macros are a starting point (40% carbs, 30% protein, 30% fat). Adjust based on your fitness goals.
Do I need to track every calorie?
Not necessarily. Use the calculation as a guide. Consistency matters more than perfection.
Is my data stored?
No. Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing is stored or shared.
