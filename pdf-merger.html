📖 What is PDF Merger?

PDF Merger is a free online tool that allows you to combine multiple PDF files into a single PDF document. Whether you have separate chapters of a book, multiple invoices, or scanned documents — our PDF Merger helps you merge them quickly and easily, right from your browser.

Unlike other online tools, KitUpBox PDF Merger does not upload your files to any server. All merging happens locally in your browser using advanced JavaScript technology. This means your files stay 100% private and secure.

  • 100% Free — No hidden charges, no signup required
  • No Watermark — Your merged PDF stays clean
  • Unlimited Files — Merge as many PDFs as you want
  • Fast Processing — Merge happens in seconds
  • Secure & Private — Files never leave your device
  • Works Everywhere — Mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop
  • No Installation — Works directly in your browser

🎯 How to Use PDF Merger

  1. Click on "Choose Files" button above
  2. Select 2 or more PDF files from your device
  3. Click "Merge & Download PDF"
  4. Your merged PDF will download automatically

💡 When Should You Use PDF Merger?

  • Combining multiple chapters of an eBook
  • Merging scanned documents into one file
  • Joining invoices or receipts together
  • Creating a single report from multiple PDFs
  • Combining resumes, certificates, or applications
  • Organizing digital documents in one place

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDF Merger really free?
Yes, 100% free. There are no hidden charges, no premium plans, and no signup required. You can merge unlimited PDFs without paying anything.
Are my PDF files safe?
Absolutely. Your files are processed directly in your browser using JavaScript. They are never uploaded to our servers, so your privacy is completely protected.
Is there a file size limit?
No specific limit, but we recommend files under 100 MB each for best performance. Very large files may take longer to process depending on your device.
Will there be a watermark on my merged PDF?
No. Unlike many free tools, KitUpBox does not add any watermark, logo, or branding to your merged PDF. Your document stays clean and professional.
Can I use this on my phone?
Yes! KitUpBox PDF Merger works perfectly on all devices — Android, iPhone, tablet, laptop, and desktop. No app installation needed.
Does it work offline?
After the page loads, the merging process works offline since it happens in your browser. However, you need an internet connection to load the page initially.
