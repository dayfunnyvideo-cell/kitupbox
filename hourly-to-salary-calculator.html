How to turn an hourly wage into a yearly salary

Many jobs in the United States pay by the hour, while job ads, loan forms and budgets often talk about a yearly salary. This calculator converts between them. It works out your yearly, monthly, every-two-weeks, weekly and daily pay from your hourly rate and the hours you work.

The formula

Yearly pay = hourly pay × hours per week × weeks per year

  • Monthly pay is the yearly pay divided by 12.
  • Pay every two weeks is the yearly pay divided by 26.
  • Weekly pay is the yearly pay divided by 52.
  • Daily pay is the hourly pay times your hours per week, divided by 5.

Worked example

At $25 an hour for 40 hours a week and 52 weeks a year, the yearly pay is $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000. That is about $4,333 a month, $2,000 every two weeks, $1,000 a week and $200 a day. A full-time year is 2,080 hours, so a quick rule is to multiply the hourly rate by 2,080.

If you take two weeks of unpaid time off and work 50 weeks, the same $25 an hour comes to $50,000 a year.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter your hourly pay.
  2. Enter the hours you work in a normal week.
  3. Enter the weeks you are paid each year. Use 52 if your vacation and holidays are paid.
  4. Press Calculate Salary to see your pay for each period.

Things to keep in mind

  • The result is before tax. Federal, state and payroll taxes, health insurance and retirement contributions are taken out, so your take-home pay is lower.
  • Overtime, bonuses and tips are not included. Add them separately if you earn them.
  • Salaried jobs often include paid time off and benefits. Compare the whole offer, not only the pay figure.

Results are estimates for planning. Check your pay stub or your employer for exact figures.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an hourly wage to a yearly salary?
Multiply your hourly pay by the hours you work each week, then by the number of weeks you are paid each year. For a full-time job that is usually hourly pay × 40 × 52, or hourly pay × 2,080.
Is the result before or after tax?
It is before tax. Your take-home pay is lower after federal, state and payroll taxes and any deductions.
How many working hours are in a year?
A standard full-time year has 40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours. If you work fewer hours or weeks, enter your own numbers.
Does this include overtime?
No. Overtime is usually paid at a higher rate and varies from week to week, so it is not included. Add it to the result yourself.
Why use 26 for pay every two weeks?
There are 52 weeks in a year, so a payment every two weeks happens 26 times a year. Some employers pay twice a month instead, which is 24 payments.
Is the calculation done on my device?
Yes. The calculation runs in your browser when you press Calculate Salary.
