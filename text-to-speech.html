📖 What is Text to Speech?

Text to Speech (TTS) is a technology that converts written text into spoken voice. It's helpful for reading long articles, learning pronunciation, accessibility, and multitasking.

✨ Key Features

  • Multiple Voices — Choose from available system voices
  • Adjustable Speed — 0.5x to 2x playback
  • Custom Pitch & Volume — Personalize the voice
  • Language Filter — Find voices in your language
  • Works Offline — After loading, uses browser voices
  • 100% Free — No signup, no limits

🎯 When to Use Text to Speech?

  • Listening to articles while multitasking
  • Learning correct pronunciation
  • Accessibility for visually impaired users
  • Studying — hear notes aloud
  • Language learning practice
  • Proofreading by listening

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Use punctuation (., ? !) for natural pauses
  • Break long text into shorter sections
  • Try different voices to find your favorite
  • Adjust speed to 0.9x for clearer pronunciation

❓ Why Use KitUpBox Text to Speech?

Uses your browser's built-in voices — no data sent to servers. Fast, private, and completely free.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many voices are available?
It depends on your device and browser. Most devices have 10-50 voices in various languages.
Why don't I hear anything?
Check your device volume. Some browsers require user interaction first. Try clicking Speak again.
Does it support Hindi?
If your device has a Hindi voice installed, yes! Filter by Hindi to see available voices.
Can I download the audio?
Currently no direct download. This uses browser voices, so recording would require screen recording tools.
Is there a text limit?
Very long text may be split by the browser. For best results, use text under 5000 characters.
Is my text saved?
No. Everything is processed in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
