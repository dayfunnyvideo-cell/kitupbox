How to Count Words and Characters (Complete Guide)

3 min read · Nov 13, 2026

Essays, cover letters, product descriptions and text messages often come with a length limit. Counting by hand is slow and easy to get wrong. This guide explains how words and characters are counted, how long common documents tend to be, and how to check your length in seconds.

How words are counted

Most word counters split your text wherever there is a space or a line break, and each piece counts as one word. A hyphenated word such as "well-known" usually counts as one word. A phrase such as "New York" counts as two. Different programs can give slightly different totals, so if a limit is strict, check your count with the tool the other side uses.

Words versus characters

Characters are every letter, number, punctuation mark and symbol. Some limits count spaces and some do not, so find out which version applies to you.

Example: "Hello, world!" has 2 words, 13 characters with spaces and 12 characters without spaces.

How many words fit on a page

The answer depends on font, size, margins and line spacing. For 12-point type with standard margins, these are common estimates:

PagesDouble-spaced
1 page250 to 300 words
2 pages500 to 600 words
5 pages1,250 to 1,500 words
10 pages2,500 to 3,000 words

Single-spaced text fits roughly twice as many words on a page.

Common length targets

  • Text message: about 160 characters of plain text fit in one standard SMS. Longer messages are split into parts.
  • Page title for search results: roughly 50 to 60 characters before it may be cut off.
  • Meta description: roughly 150 to 160 characters.
  • Cover letter: often one page, around 250 to 400 words.
  • College application essay: the Common App personal essay has a 650-word maximum. Check the current instructions before you submit.

Search engines cut titles by width, not by a fixed character count, so treat the search figures as guidelines.

Speaking and reading time

At a comfortable pace most people speak about 130 words a minute and read silently at about 200 to 250 words a minute.

Speaking timeWords at 130 a minute
1 minute130 words
5 minutes650 words
10 minutes1,300 words

A 1,000-word article takes about 4 to 5 minutes to read.

Tips for staying within a limit

  • Write a full first draft, then cut. It is easier to trim than to stretch.
  • Delete filler such as "very", "really" and "in order to".
  • Replace long phrases with short ones. "Because" beats "due to the fact that".
  • Remove sentences that repeat an earlier point.
  • Read the text aloud. Wordy parts are easy to hear.

Common mistakes

  • Counting the wrong thing. Check whether the limit is in words or characters.
  • Ignoring what is included. Some limits include headings, footnotes or references and some do not.
  • Trusting text copied from a PDF. Stray line breaks can change the count.
  • Counting once, then editing. Count again after your final changes.

Frequently asked questions

Do spaces count as characters?

It depends on the limit. Many platforms count spaces, and some forms count only visible characters. Look for the words "with spaces" or "without spaces".

Does a hyphenated word count as one word or two?

Most counters treat it as one word. A few programs may treat it differently, so check if the limit is strict.

How many words is 500 words?

About one single-spaced page, or about two double-spaced pages. It takes roughly 4 minutes to speak at 130 words a minute.
