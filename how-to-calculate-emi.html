How to Calculate Percentages (Complete Guide)

3 min read · Nov 12, 2026

Percentages show up in prices, sales tax, tips, grades and pay raises. The math is simple once you know three formulas. This guide walks through each one with examples you can try yourself.

What a percentage is

A percentage is a number out of 100. So 25% means 25 out of 100, which is 0.25 as a decimal. To use a percentage in a calculation, divide it by 100 first.

Formula 1: Find a percentage of a number

Result = Number × Percent ÷ 100
Example: What is 15% of $80? 80 × 15 ÷ 100 = $12.

This is the formula behind tips. A 20% tip on a $45 dinner is 45 × 20 ÷ 100 = $9, so the total is $54. For a quick estimate in your head, move the decimal one place to find 10%, then double it for 20%.

Formula 2: What percent is one number of another

Percent = Part ÷ Whole × 100
Example: You score 42 out of 50 on a test. 42 ÷ 50 × 100 = 84%.

Formula 3: Percent change

Percent change = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100
Example: Your rent goes from $1,200 to $1,284. (1,284 − 1,200) ÷ 1,200 × 100 = 7% increase.

A positive result is an increase and a negative result is a decrease. Always divide by the old value, not the new one.

Discounts and sales tax

Discount

Sale price = Original price × (1 − Discount ÷ 100)
Example: A $60 jacket is 30% off. 60 × 0.70 = $42, so you save $18.

Sales tax

Total = Price × (1 + Tax rate ÷ 100)
Example: The $42 jacket with 8% sales tax costs 42 × 1.08 = $45.36.

Two discounts in a row

Discounts do not simply add up. Take 20% off, then another 10% off. You pay 80% of the price, then 90% of that, which is 72% of the original. The total discount is 28%, not 30%.

Common mistakes

  • Using the wrong base. For percent change, divide by the starting value.
  • Treating increases and decreases as equal. A price that rises 50% and then falls 50% does not return to where it started. $100 becomes $150, then $75.
  • Forgetting to convert. Use 0.08, not 8, when you multiply by a rate.
  • Mixing up percent and percentage points. A rate that moves from 4% to 5% rose by 1 percentage point, which is a 25% increase.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the original price after a discount?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount). If a jacket costs $42 after 30% off, the original price is 42 ÷ 0.70 = $60.

How do I add a percentage to a number?

Multiply the number by (1 + percent ÷ 100). To add 8% to $42, multiply by 1.08.

How do I turn a percentage into a decimal?

Divide by 100, or move the decimal point two places to the left. 7.5% becomes 0.075.
