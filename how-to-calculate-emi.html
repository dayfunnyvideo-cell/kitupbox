How to Calculate Loan EMI (Complete Guide)

If you are planning to take a home loan, car loan, or personal loan, the most important term you will hear is EMI. Understanding how EMI works and how it is calculated can help you plan your finances better.

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount of money you pay to the bank every month to repay your loan. Each EMI consists of two parts:

  • Principal: The actual amount you borrowed.
  • Interest: The cost of borrowing the money.

In the initial months, your EMI mostly goes towards the interest, and later it shifts towards the principal amount.

The EMI Formula

Banks use a standard formula to calculate your EMI:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ - 1)
  • P = Principal loan amount
  • r = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate / 12 / 100)
  • n = Loan tenure in months

While you can calculate this manually, it is much easier to use an online calculator.

Tips to Reduce Your EMI

  • Make a larger down payment: This reduces the principal amount.
  • Choose a longer tenure: This reduces the monthly EMI, but you will pay more interest overall.
  • Improve your credit score: A good CIBIL score can help you get a lower interest rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a longer tenure mean less EMI?

Yes, a longer tenure spreads your loan over more months, reducing the monthly EMI, but it increases the total interest paid.

Can I prepay my loan?

Yes, most banks allow prepayment. It helps you save on interest, but check if there are any prepayment charges.

Is the EMI fixed for the entire tenure?

For fixed-rate loans, yes. For floating-rate loans, the EMI can change if the interest rate changes.
