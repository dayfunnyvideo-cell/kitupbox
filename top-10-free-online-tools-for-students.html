Top 10 Free Online Tools for Students (2026)

Being a student in 2026 means juggling assignments, projects, and exams. Having the right set of online tools can save you hours of work and help you stay organized. Here are the top 10 free online tools every student should bookmark.

1. PDF Merger

Combining multiple PDF files into one is a daily task for students. Whether it's submitting assignments or combining notes, our PDF Merger tool makes it instant and secure.

2. Image to PDF Converter

Need to submit photos of your handwritten notes? Use the Image to PDF tool to convert multiple JPG/PNG images into a single PDF.
