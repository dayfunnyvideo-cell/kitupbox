📖 What is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a number worked out from your height and weight. Doctors and health organizations use it as a quick screening tool to see whether a weight is low, healthy or high for a person's height. It is a starting point for a conversation with a doctor, not a diagnosis.

✨ What this calculator does

  • Pounds or kilograms: use US units (feet, inches, pounds) or metric units (cm, kg).
  • Standard adult categories: underweight, normal weight, overweight and obese.
  • Healthy weight range: the weights that give a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 at your height.
  • Instant and free: the result appears when you press the button, with no sign-up.

🎯 BMI categories for adults

  • Under 18.5: underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9: normal (healthy) weight
  • 25 to 29.9: overweight
  • 30 and above: obese

💡 How BMI is calculated

Metric: BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ (height in meters)2

US units: BMI = 703 × weight in pounds ÷ (height in inches)2

Example in metric: a person who weighs 70 kg and is 1.75 m tall has a BMI of 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.9, which is normal weight.

Example in US units: a person who is 5 feet 8 inches (68 inches) tall and weighs 160 pounds has a BMI of 703 × 160 ÷ (68 × 68) = 112,480 ÷ 4,624 = 24.3, which is normal weight. At 5 feet 8 inches, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 matches a weight of about 122 to 164 pounds.

🎯 When is a BMI calculator useful?

  • Checking where your weight sits for your height
  • Starting a fitness or eating plan and noting your starting point
  • Tracking weight changes over time
  • Getting ready for a doctor visit

⚠️ What BMI cannot tell you

  • It does not separate muscle from fat. Athletes and people with a lot of muscle can have a high BMI without being overweight.
  • It does not show where fat is carried on the body, and body fat around the waist matters for health.
  • It can read differently for older adults, who often lose muscle.
  • For children and teens (ages 2 to 19), BMI is read against growth charts for the child's age and sex, so the adult categories above do not apply. Ask a pediatrician.
  • It is not used the same way during pregnancy.

Because of these limits, doctors look at BMI together with other things such as waist size, blood tests, diet, activity and family history. If your result worries you, talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?
For most adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is considered the normal weight range.
How do I calculate BMI in pounds?
Multiply your weight in pounds by 703, then divide by your height in inches squared. This calculator does it for you when you choose US units.
Is BMI accurate for everyone?
No. BMI does not tell muscle from fat, so very muscular people can score high. It is a screening tool, and a doctor can look at the full picture.
Can I use this BMI calculator for my child?
No. For children and teens, BMI is compared with growth charts for their age and sex. Ask a pediatrician to read it.
Does BMI differ for men and women?
The adult categories are the same for men and women, but body fat is spread differently. That is one reason BMI alone is not a full health check.
What should I do if my BMI is high or low?
Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian. They can look at your health as a whole and suggest food and activity changes that suit you.
What is the difference between BMI and body fat percentage?
BMI uses only height and weight. Body fat percentage estimates how much of your weight is fat, but it needs special tools to measure.
Is my data stored?
No. The calculation runs in your browser when you press the button. This page does not save what you type.
