📖 What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple measure that uses your height and weight to categorize whether you're at a healthy weight. It's used by doctors and health organizations worldwide as a screening tool.

🎯 BMI Categories

  • 18.5 – 24.9 — Normal (healthy) weight
  • 25 – 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30 and above — Obese

💡 How BMI is Calculated

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (meters)

Example: 70 kg ÷ (1.75 m)² = 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.86 (Normal weight)

🎯 When to Use BMI Calculator?

  • Health screening before doctor visits

⚠️ Important Note

BMI is a general screening tool, not a diagnostic tool. It doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat. Athletes and bodybuilders may have high BMI without being overweight. Always consult a doctor for personalized advice.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?
A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy for most adults, according to WHO.
Is BMI accurate for everyone?
BMI doesn't differentiate between muscle and fat. Athletes may have higher BMI. For most people, it's a useful indicator.
Does BMI differ by gender?
The same BMI categories apply to men and women, but body fat distribution differs. Children use age-specific charts.
What should I do if my BMI is high?
Consult a doctor or nutritionist. They can recommend diet, exercise, and lifestyle changes suited to you.
What's the difference between BMI and body fat?
BMI is based on height and weight only. Body fat percentage measures actual fat tissue — more accurate but harder to measure.
