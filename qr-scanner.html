📖 What is QR Code Scanner?

QR Code Scanner is a free online tool that reads QR codes from images. Simply upload a photo or screenshot containing a QR code, and our tool will instantly decode it and show you the stored information.

Everything happens locally in your browser using advanced JavaScript — your images never leave your device.

✨ Key Features

  • 100% Free — No signup, no charges
  • Image Upload — Scan from any photo
  • Instant Result — Decoded in seconds
  • Copy to Clipboard — One-click copy
  • Secure & Private — Files stay on device
  • Works Everywhere — All devices

🎯 How to Use QR Scanner

  1. Take a screenshot or photo of a QR code
  2. Upload the image above
  3. Click "Scan QR Code"
  4. View the decoded result

💡 When Should You Use It?

  • Scanning QR codes from screenshots
  • Reading QR codes from photos
  • When your phone camera can't scan
  • Extracting URLs from QR images
  • Verifying QR code content

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which image formats work?
All common formats: JPG, PNG, JPEG, BMP, and more. Just upload any image containing a QR code.
Is my image safe?
Yes, 100%. Your image is processed locally in your browser — never uploaded to any server.
What if the QR code can't be scanned?
Make sure the image is clear, well-lit, and the QR code is fully visible. Blurry or partially hidden QR codes may not work.
Can I scan QR codes from the camera?
Currently we support image upload. Take a photo/screenshot first, then upload it. Camera scanning may come in a future update.
Does it work with colored QR codes?
Yes, our scanner handles colored QR codes as long as there's enough contrast between foreground and background.
Can I scan multiple QR codes at once?
Currently we scan one QR code per image. Upload images one at a time.
