📖 What is Image Resizer?

An Image Resizer lets you change the dimensions (width and height) of an image. Whether you need to reduce file size, fit a specific size requirement, or prepare images for web/print, this tool makes it easy.

✨ Key Features

  • Custom Dimensions — Set any width/height
  • Aspect Ratio Lock — Keep proportions correct
  • Multiple Formats — JPG, PNG, WebP
  • Instant Preview — See result before download
  • Secure & Private — Files never leave your device
  • 100% Free — No signup, no limits

🎯 When to Use Image Resizer?

  • Meeting website upload requirements
  • Preparing photos for social media
  • Reducing image size for emails
  • Matching specific print dimensions
  • Creating thumbnails
  • Standardizing multiple images

💡 Common Image Sizes

  • Instagram Post — 1080 × 1080 px
  • Facebook Cover — 820 × 312 px
  • YouTube Thumbnail — 1280 × 720 px
  • Passport Photo — 600 × 600 px
  • Website Hero — 1920 × 1080 px

❓ Why Use KitUpBox Image Resizer?

Fast, accurate, and completely free. Your images are processed locally in your browser — they never get uploaded to any server.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?
We support all common formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP.
Will my image lose quality?
Reducing size keeps most quality. Enlarging may cause slight blurriness — this is normal for all resizing tools.
What does "Maintain aspect ratio" do?
It automatically adjusts the other dimension to keep your image from looking stretched or squished.
Is my image safe?
Yes, 100%. Images are processed in your browser — never uploaded.
Can I resize multiple images?
Currently one image at a time. Batch resizing may come in future updates.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes! Works perfectly on Android, iPhone, and tablets.
