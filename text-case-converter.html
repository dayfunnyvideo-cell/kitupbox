📖 What is a text case converter?

A text case converter changes the capital letters in your text. Paste a sentence typed in ALL CAPS, press a button, and it becomes normal text again. It can also turn a heading into Title Case or turn a phrase into a name that programmers use, like camelCase or snake_case. It saves you from retyping text by hand.

How to use the text case converter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Press the case you want. The text in the box changes right away.
  3. Press Undo if you want the previous text back, or Copy Text to copy the result.

✨ The cases and what they do

  • UPPERCASE: every letter is a capital. "hello world" becomes "HELLO WORLD".
  • lowercase: every letter is small. "Hello World" becomes "hello world".
  • Title Case: headings in the style most English writers use. Small words such as "a", "the", "of" and "and" stay lowercase unless they come first or last. "a tale of two cities" becomes "A Tale of Two Cities".
  • Sentence case: the first letter of each sentence is a capital and the rest is small. It also fixes a lone "i" to "I". "hELLO THERE. i am here" becomes "Hello there. I am here".
  • Capitalize Each Word: every word starts with a capital, with no exceptions. "a tale of two cities" becomes "A Tale Of Two Cities".
  • aLtErNaTiNg: letters switch between small and capital. "hello world" becomes "hElLo WoRlD". People use it for jokes and memes.
  • camelCase: words joined together with no spaces, and each word after the first starts with a capital. "user first name" becomes "userFirstName".
  • snake_case: small words joined with underscores. "user first name" becomes "user_first_name".
  • kebab-case: small words joined with hyphens. "user first name" becomes "user-first-name".

🎯 When a case converter helps

  • Fixing text that was typed with Caps Lock on
  • Formatting headlines, blog titles and email subject lines
  • Cleaning up text copied from a document that came out in all capitals
  • Naming variables, files and web page addresses in code and on websites
  • Making captions and posts

Title Case and style guides

There is no single rule for Title Case. Style guides such as AP, Chicago and APA differ on which small words stay lowercase and on words of four letters or more. This tool uses a common approach: it capitalizes the first and last word and every important word, and leaves short articles, conjunctions and prepositions in lowercase. If you must follow one style guide exactly, check the result against it.

Tips

  • The converter lowercases the text first when it makes Title Case or Sentence case. Names and acronyms such as "NASA" or "iPhone" may need to be fixed by hand afterwards.
  • Use Undo if a result is not what you wanted.
  • Sentence case treats a period followed by a space as the end of a sentence, so abbreviations such as "e.g." may be followed by a capital letter. Check those by eye.
  • For web addresses and file names, kebab-case is a good choice because it is easy to read and avoids spaces.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalize Each Word?
Title Case follows common headline rules and leaves small words like "of" and "the" in lowercase, except at the start or end. Capitalize Each Word capitalizes every word.
What is Sentence case?
Sentence case capitalizes the first letter of each sentence and keeps the rest lowercase. Example: "This is sentence case. It reads like normal writing."
How do I fix text typed in all capitals?
Paste it in the box and press Sentence case for normal writing, or lowercase if you want no capitals at all.
What are camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case?
They are ways to write several words as one name. camelCase joins words and capitalizes each new word, snake_case joins small words with underscores, and kebab-case joins them with hyphens.
Does it keep acronyms like NASA?
No. Title Case and Sentence case lowercase the text first, so acronyms and names may need fixing by hand.
Can I get my old text back?
Yes. Press Undo to go back to the text before the last change.
Is my text saved?
No. The conversion runs in your browser, and this page does not send or save what you type.
Is there a text limit?
This page has no set limit. Very large texts may make your browser slow.
