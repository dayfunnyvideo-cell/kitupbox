📖 What is a Text Case Converter?

A Text Case Converter transforms text between different capitalization styles — UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, and more. Perfect for writers, editors, students, and anyone working with text.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Title Case?
Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word. Example: "This Is Title Case".
What is Sentence case?
Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of the first word. Example: "This is sentence case."
What is Alternating case?
Alternating case switches between upper and lower for each letter. Example: "aLtErNaTiNg".
Is my text saved?
No. Everything happens in your browser. Your text is never uploaded.
Is there a text limit?
No specific limit. Paste as much text as you want.
